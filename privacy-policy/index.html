Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 27 May 2026

At Kroot, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and login credentials (including social login data if you sign in via Apple, Google, or Facebook).
  • App Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the app, such as your study progress, flashcard creation, and the badges you earn.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic information about your device, such as your operating system, device model, and IP address, to help us identify and fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the app's functionality.
  • Sync your flashcards and study progress across your devices.
  • Process your subscription payments securely (handled by third-party payment providers; we do not store your credit card details).
  • Send you important app updates, reminders, and support responses.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services that help us run the app (such as cloud hosting, analytics, or payment processors).
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Account Deletion: You can request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us or using the in-app deletion feature (if available).
  • Notifications: You can opt out of push notifications or promotional emails at any time through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Kroot is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any major changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at: admin@krootapp.com
