About Us

Welcome to Kroot, your study companion for medical excellence.

Built by and for healthcare professionals, Kroot is designed to make mastering complex medical concepts simple, efficient, and engaging. We understand the rigorous demands of medical training, which is why we created a flashcard-based platform for building, organizing, and reviewing the material you need to know.

What you can do in Kroot

  • Browse flashcard decks organized by topic, exam, and academic year
  • Create your own decks and add flashcards with questions, answers, and images
  • Work through cards and rate how well you recalled each one
  • Track your study activity over time and earn badges as you build consistent habits
  • Rate and review decks, and see what other users thought before you start one
  • Earn from decks you create, and withdraw your earnings to your bank account

Our mission is to help medical students, doctors, and healthcare practitioners study smarter, not harder. Whether you are preparing for board exams, refreshing your clinical knowledge, or building a custom library of study materials, Kroot provides the tools you need to succeed.

Kroot is a study tool for learning and exam preparation. It is not a substitute for professional medical training, clinical judgment, or patient care.
