About Us

Welcome to Kroot, your ultimate study companion for medical excellence.

Built by and for healthcare professionals, Kroot is designed to make mastering complex medical concepts simple, efficient, and engaging. We understand the rigorous demands of medical training, which is why we created a smart, flashcard-based platform powered by spaced repetition technology.

Our mission is to help medical students, doctors, and healthcare practitioners study smarter, not harder. Whether you are preparing for board exams, refreshing your clinical knowledge, or building a custom library of study materials, Kroot provides the tools you need to succeed.

Empower your medical journey with Kroot. Learn faster, remember longer, and achieve your goals.
